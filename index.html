Hello, I'm Rohan.

I started The Corner Cup in 2019 after helping my mother in her tiffin kitchen. She taught me that people remember how a place feels before they remember the food.

Chai at my nani's house in Bareilly is where my coffee habit began, and I wanted a space where students, freelancers and families could all sit comfortably without being rushed.

  • Table spots with charging points for laptop work
  • Desserts baked in small batches every morning
  • Outdoor seating for evenings
  • No-contact delivery, packed hot
  • Pay what you see on the board, no surprise charges
